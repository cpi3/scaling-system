Dactyl Cygnus — Custom Split Ergonomic Keyboard

€369 wired  /  €399 wireless Switches and keycaps not included. Premium options may affect final price.

I make fully custom Dactyl split keyboards, built to order and made by hand. Everything is configurable: layout and size, color, surface texture, finish or clear coat, switches, and keycaps. Each keyboard is made specifically for the buyer.

The cases are not raw 3D prints. Every case goes through a full finishing process: sanding, filler primer, and coating. This removes visible print lines and gives a clean, uniform surface that feels intentional and finished — not “3D printed.”

Each keyboard is hand-wired, assembled, and tested individually. The price reflects the time required for the full process, including customization, finishing, and manual wiring.

If you want a Dactyl that’s built cleanly, looks good on your desk, and is made exactly to your preferences — this is it. Message me before ordering and we’ll go through the details together. :)

Note: The €369/€399 price is for a base wired/wireless build. Switches and keycaps are not included. The shown configuration adds €50.

The keyboard shown has a textured painted finish. This is optional — smooth finish is available too. All finishes, colors, and surface treatments are customizable.

Free shipping for all EU countries.

The process — what goes into each keyboard

StepWhat happens
1. ConsultationWe talk about your layout, color, surface, switches, keycaps, and any other preferences.
2. 3D printingThe case is printed to your configuration. Print settings are tuned for structural quality.
3. FinishingEvery surface is sanded, sprayed with filler primer, sanded again, and coated. Print lines are gone.
4. Hand wiringEvery switch is wired by hand — no PCB. This takes time but gives full flexibility on layout.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to program a keyboard?
No. I flash the firmware before shipping. The keyboard works out of the box. If you want to remap keys later, QMK/ZMK both have easy tools for that.
What switches do you recommend?
Totally up to you. Tactile (e.g. Boba U4, Gazzew) and linear (e.g. Gateron Yellow) are popular choices. Ask me if unsure.
What keycap profiles work with a Dactyl?
DSA, XDA, and MT3 all work well. Cherry profile is generally not recommended for curved keywells.
